Race plan: Apalachee Regional Park 5K

From the coaches' strategy talk on Nov 16. Two loops, about 5K, net downhill. FHSAA Class 2A boys race: Friday, Nov 20, 8:35 AM.

Hills = maintain rhythm Tops of hills = reset Downhills = press

The 30-second version

  1. Start to 1K: quick but controlled. Target in front of you, within reach. Let fast starters burn away.
  2. ~1.4K speed bump to the mile: use the downhill to close the gap. Run tangents. Don't get boxed.
  3. The Wall (1st): don't press it. Maintain rhythm, stay attached.
  4. Top of the Wall: reset. Coach Chris will be yelling it.
  5. Downhill into the 2-mile: take the lead of your group and press. Steady gas, not a sprint.
  6. 2-mile hill: maintain rhythm. Keep any gap you opened.
  7. After the 2-mile: reset again.
  8. 3.5K to 4K downhill: press. Open the gap or catch the next group.
  9. Final Wall and finish: who wants it more.

How each job works

Keep in reach
Your target likely goes out faster than you. That's the plan. You can see him, a few steps up, but you didn't match his start.
Close the gap
Let the downhill do the work. Make contact before the Wall.
Maintain rhythm
It looks slower; that's smart. Don't slow down while they speed up. Reset faster at the top instead.
Reset
Your heart rate just spiked and your legs hurt. Normal. Breathe, shake out the shoulders, relax the face.
Press
Not a head-down surge. Slowly push the gas and let the downhill carry you, leading your group.
Don't get stuck
Behind someone who isn't your target? Get around him and move up to the group you want.

Why it works

  • Teams go out too fast here every year. Our advantage is being smart and catching people.
  • Right after the Wall everyone wants to relax on the downhill. That's exactly when we press.
  • Making someone who went out too fast run a little faster downhill, then up a hill, is how you break him.

Packs

  • Kayaan races the front and keeps Ches and Chandler behind him.
  • Emilio + Adrian work together on Sharma, then Chandler.
  • Nicolas bridges up to Rigdon and Cleek.
  • Tyler + Christopher move up through the 16:16–16:29 pack. Barragan and Sumner are landmarks there, worth 1 point each.
  • Aston hangs on to Tyler and Christopher.
